About the Game

OBJECTIVE

Guide your crab through the ocean and catch as much food as possible while avoiding ocean pollution. Reach 50 points to descend into the deep sea where more valuable food awaits! But be careful - if you consume 50 points worth of trash, your crab will die from pollution poisoning!

INSTRUCTIONS

Controls: Use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move your crab horizontally.

Gameplay:

  • Catch food items to gain points (different foods are worth different amounts)
  • Avoid trash items - they reduce your score and add to your pollution meter
  • Watch the red trash bar at the top - if it fills completely, you die!
  • At 50 points, you'll enter Level 2 (the deep sea) with new, more valuable food types
  • Your crab gets faster as you score more points
  • Press H at any time to return to the character selection screen
  • Press R to restart after dying
